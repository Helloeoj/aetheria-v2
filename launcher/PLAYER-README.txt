Aetheria Closed Beta

Extract the complete launcher download into a folder, then open
AetheriaLauncher.exe. Keep all extracted files together, including butler.exe,
7z.dll, c7zip.dll and launcher.json.

Claim your personal invitation on itch.io first. Sign into that same itch.io
account in the launcher, choose Check / update, then Play. Updates and an exact
approved game version are required. Downloads are capped at 500,000 bytes/sec.

Use your separate Aetheria game account on the game's login screen. New game
accounts require the single-use beta code supplied by the developer. Your
character progress is saved on the game server.

If the host PC is offline or updating, wait and check again later. Report gameplay
issues to the developer. Sentinel access is separate from beta access.
