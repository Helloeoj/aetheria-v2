# Starforged dependency audit

2026-10-26. Read-only audit of the active working tree (HEAD `66dbe5d` at final
inspection), including existing local changes. Paths below are repository-relative.
Only this report was written; no gameplay/data changes or live-save inspection.

Approved direction: all Starforged equipment is drop-exclusive; no Starforged
crafting/smithing; pickaxes are rare drops only; Ore/Ingot cease being obtainable
resources or crafting materials. Preserve usable equipment and save compatibility.

- **Equipment IDs — `godot_client/data/items.json`:** 180 Dagger, 181 Shortsword, 182 Longsword, 183 Mace, 184 Warmaul, 185 Bow, 186 Crossbow; 189 Helmet, 190 Platebody, 191 Platelegs, 192 Kiteshield, 193 Square Shield; 195 Ring, 196 Necklace. All are tier 7. Only Ring/Necklace currently explicitly set `drop_only: true`, `craftable: false`; retain IDs, stats and slots.

- **Resource/component IDs — same catalog:** 256 Starforged Ore, 257 Starforged Ingot, 258 Starforged Arrowheads, 259 Starforged Bolts, 328 Starforged Arrows. All exclude generic loot and carry `boss_material`; 257/258/259/328 still say `craftable: true`. Ore description still advertises smelting/future gathering. Crossbow Limbs exist only as a planned recipe output, not an item. Ammunition/components also need the no-Starforged-crafting rule; their future drop supply is unresolved.

- **Historical names are not all Starforged items:** `godot_client/scripts/game_data.gd` resolves Runite equipment aliases to the retained IDs. Old “Starforged Magic Staff”, “Starforged Magic Wand” and “Starforged Cape” instead resolve to Celestial IDs 187, 188 and 194; 188 is a retained legacy placeholder. Avoid substring-based bans that accidentally disable Celestial crafting or break these aliases.

- **17 recipe dependencies — `godot_client/data/recipes.json`:** 11 enabled: `smelt_starforged` (one Ore → one Ingot) and `starforged_{dagger,shortsword,longsword,mace,warmaul,helmet,platebody,platelegs,kiteshield,square_shield}`. Six disabled but still planned: `starforged_{arrowheads,bolts,arrows,bow,crossbow_limbs,crossbow}`. Both active and pending entries contradict the new direction; disable/remove manufacturing routes without deleting finished equipment.

- **Craft enforcement:** `godot_client/scripts/crafting_service.gd` → `craft()` / `_output_allowed()` checks recipe enablement, but not general `craftable`/`drop_only` flags or Starforged material. Changing flags alone cannot stop manufacturing. `godot_client/scripts/ui_crafting.gd` lists/calls recipes; `godot_client/network/authoritative_region.gd` handles online `craft` through the same service. Client filtering alone is insufficient.

- **Smithing/art coupling:** `godot_client/scripts/smithing_catalog.gd` includes Starforged in `METALS`, categorizes Ore/Ingot and ten finished equipment forms, and feeds the F6 list in `godot_client/scripts/ui_item_menu.gd`. `godot_client/scripts/item_visual_library.gd` also uses that category for equipment placeholder icons. Removing a metal from the smithing list can therefore remove retained gear visuals unless classification/presentation remains supported. No exact Starforged/Runite entries were found in `godot_client/data/item_visuals.json` or `equipment_visuals.json`.

- **Acquisition policy conflict:** `godot_client/data/acquisition.json` grants Golem Titan-only eligibility to 256/257/258/259/328, all marked `supply_pending`; `godot_client/scripts/acquisition_rules.gd::can_drop()` does not treat that flag as a prohibition. Its `_is_top_material()` protects tier-7 crafting/ammunition/boss materials, not ordinary Starforged equipment. Unconfigured equipment defaults to drop-eligible; a broad tier-7 denial would risk removing intended equipment drops. Ore/Ingot need explicit no-source treatment, not merely `generic_loot: false`.

- **Actual drop gap:** `godot_client/data/loot.json` contains no Starforged or Runite named outcomes and no Golem Titan table. `godot_client/scripts/loot_system.gd` uses explicit common/rare tables or tier-based fallback; `godot_client/data/monsters.json` tops out at tier 5 (Titan 1029 is tier 5), so fallback cannot yield tier-7 equipment. `godot_client/docs/GolemLootReview.md` records intended rare Titan Runite/Starforged equipment, but odds remain unimplemented. Preserve that intended route while acknowledging that usable catalog gear does not yet have a verified live drop source. Catalog `rarity: common` is not a drop probability.

- **Pickaxes/Mining:** no pickaxe item exists in `godot_client/data/items.json`; `godot_client/data/mining.json` has `pickaxes: []` and no Starforged deposit. `godot_client/scripts/mining_definitions.gd` requires real catalog tool IDs; `acquisition_rules.gd::can_mine()` and `tools/mining_balance.py` already exclude Starforged Ore. A rare Starforged pickaxe needs approved item identity, eligible drop source and odds—not invented IDs or an ore recipe. Candidate Runite rock art in `art_sources/ore-nodes/README.md` / `expansion-provenance.json` is unbound source art, not authorization for Starforged resources.

- **Salvage:** focused searches found no active salvage/dismantle/recycling implementation in Godot, backend or tools. `godot_client/docs/Crafting.md` describes pending salvage; `GolemLootReview.md` mentions proposed ingot salvage. Future salvage must not recreate Starforged Ore/Ingot or provide a manufacturing loop; no existing salvage reward table needs editing now.

- **Shops, starters, Orders:** `godot_client/scripts/shop_service.gd` only sells policy-approved fishing poles/bait. `godot_client/scripts/main.gd` and `godot_client/network/character_codec.gd::initial()` give rod/bait, not Starforged. `godot_client/scripts/orders/orders_definitions.gd` restricts rewards to approved low-tier supplies; `godot_client/data/dead_man_orders.json` keeps high-tier cosmetic concepts deferred. `orders_directives.gd` resolves equipment/crafting targets against actual items/recipes, so later recipe retirement must not leave enabled directives pointing to removed recipes. No current Starforged grant was found there.

- **Generators/transfers:** offline F6 `ui_item_menu.gd::_give_item()` can grant Ore/Ingot and gear by ID through Smithing/All Items; online grants are denied. `game_data.gd::register_item()` is generic registration, with no active Starforged generator found. `inventory_system.gd` / `player_state.gd` additions do not enforce acquisition policy; existing stacks can also be dropped/picked up through `authoritative_region.gd`. Blocking future loot alone neither removes old materials nor prevents their circulation. Decide whether retired materials remain inert, migrate, or are removed with compensation; do not silently destroy inventories or redesign the dev UI.

- **Critical save risk:** `godot_client/network/character_codec.gd` stores inventory/equipment as item IDs; unknown inventory IDs reject the whole character with `save_inventory`, not just the missing stack. Deleting 256/257 outright can prevent affected characters loading. `game_data.gd` and `inventory_system.gd::normalize_backpack()` preserve name aliases, but are not a retirement migration. Retain inert compatibility definitions or implement an explicit tested migration; never reuse IDs. `backend/accounts/models.py` stores both Character and Journal JSON; `backend/accounts/views.py::validate_state()` validates structure, not catalog existence, so it cannot repair this. Existing player counts/holdings were not queried; backup, live-state and recovery-journal handling remain rollout requirements.

- **Preserve equipment usability:** `godot_client/scripts/inventory_system.gd`, `equipment_progression.gd` and `equipment_utils.gd` handle equipping, requirements and weapon behavior independently of crafting routes. Keep Starforged stats/slots, Runite aliases and tier-7 level-60 equipment requirements intact. Preserve historical equipment saves even if originally crafted; acquisition changes do not justify confiscation or changing combat behavior.

- **Tests/docs to update with implementation:** contradictory expectations live in `godot_client/tests/catalog_acquisition_smoke.gd` (Titan Ore eligibility), `godot_client/scripts/dev_tools/smithing_catalog_smoke.gd` and `bronze_recipes_smoke.gd` (Starforged crafting/catalog). Retain alias/equipment coverage in `crafting_ui_smoke.gd`, `runite_equipment_smoke.gd`, `titan_balance_smoke.gd`; add retirement-save and no-manufacturing/no-resource-source cases to the existing crafting/persistence suites. Superseded rules remain in `godot_client/docs/Crafting.md`, `CraftingAcquisition.md`, `docs/mining/{discovery,design,implementation-plan}.md`, and `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-23-smithing-catalog-menus.md`. No gameplay tests were run for this documentation-only audit.
