# Bound Titan — native skeletal idle and approved grounded slam

The user selected subtle chest and arm movement, then rejected the rigid bitmap-section approach and explicitly requested a skeleton. The selected idle now uses an actual Godot Skeleton2D, 25 Bone2Ds and one continuous weighted mesh. Spine, neck, shoulders, elbows and wrists articulate independently while the pelvis, legs and magical bindings stay fixed. The fluid ground slam is user-approved and remains unchanged. These sources do not change the production actor catalog, map, collision, camera or gameplay.

## Current clips

- **Idle:** native weighted skeleton, 2.4-second continuous cycle. The chest rises by at most 1.3 native pixels; complete fists follow an approximately 2.12-pixel arc through separate upper-arm, forearm and wrist controls. Delayed joint tracks provide follow-through. The one unchanged source texture is attached to 4,225 shared mesh vertices / 8,192 triangles, with at most four normalized local bone influences per vertex. The source is not split into moving bitmap sections. Forty-eight actual native GPU samples at 50ms retain a lossless export; the main map preview renders the live skeleton directly. Pelvis, knees, feet, bindings and anchors remain pixel-exact, and the full cycle returns exactly to the neutral pose. User artwork approval remains pending.
- **Ground slam:** 18 complete frames, 2.31-second preview cycle. The upper body hinges forward and both complete fists reach the floor. No horizontal body strips, frozen knees or partially reused hands. Only measured opaque terrain-mat colors beneath the contact poses were removed from alpha; the articulated body poses remain intact.

Each clip has lossless `frames/*.png`, a horizontal PNG sprite sheet, an engine/service `final-export.png` sheet and preview GIFs. `south.png` is the shared neutral pose. `animation-source.json` records the selected skeletal implementation, source hashes, bone hierarchy, native sample timing and unchanged slam provenance. Rejected bitmap-part motion and the unselected core pulse are recorded separately. Unmodified slam generation sources remain under `shockwave_slam/temporal_strike/raw` and `temporal_recovery/raw`.

The actual rig is `godot_client/scripts/dev_tools/golem_titan_skeleton_rig.gd` (repository-relative), with the byte-identical whole source at `godot_client/Assets/Characters/GolemTitanBoundCandidate/south.png`. Instantiate it at world anchor minus [128,240], call `sample_at_seconds(t)`, and use `set_debug_skeleton(true)` for the actual bone overlay. `get_rig_report()` exposes hierarchy and weight checks. Sampling is deterministic; the caller owns playback. Legs and magical bindings use fixed controls and pinned mesh supports. Superseded cutout recipes/layers remain in Git history and local rejected-source archives.

## Review evidence and limits

Native canvas: 256 × 256; candidate density: 1 world pixel/source pixel; anchor: [128, 240]. The floor contact pose is checked against the unchanged Golem Kingdom paving and the actual peasant player at its unchanged 0.75 scale.

The dedicated review tool instantiates the actual content scene and candidate actor in memory. It samples the real floor/collision geometry for the temporary review position and captures every frame through an actual GPU Camera2D at zoom 1 and 4, plus normal 0.68 samples. No characters are baked into a map image. No production placement/collision acceptance is inferred from these screenshots or a proposed review footprint.

The selected skeletal idle has 105 actual GPU captures with zero failures: all 48 live poses at Camera2D zoom 1 and 4, context/guide captures and visible bone overlays. All 48 actual 4× poses were independently inspected for complete fists, elbow/wrist articulation, shoulder/waist joins, armor volumes, fixed restraints and map clipping. No detached joint, large cutout seam or gross armor deformation was observed. Separate native GPU checks verify the original palette, binary alpha, clear borders, one connected silhouette per frame, fixed lower-body pixels and exact source-neutral/full-cycle equality. Lossless animated WebP playback reproduces the direct live-skeleton map renders and durations exactly; identical consecutive poses may be combined by the encoder.

The user-approved grounded slam retains its prior actual-map review from the 62-capture run. All 18 selected slam frames remain unchanged. Both animations share the complete source neutral pose.

Evidence is outside Git under `work/character-animation/golem-titan-bound-v2/evidence`: `godot-skeleton-rig` contains native bakes, endpoint and structural proof; `godot-live-skeleton-idle` contains the selected direct map renders, independent inspection, bone overlays and animated-preview QA; `godot-rigid-idle-ground-slam` contains the retained approved slam.
