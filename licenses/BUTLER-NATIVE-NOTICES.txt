Official butler v15.32.0 Windows runtime

The unchanged butler.exe, 7z.dll and c7zip.dll come from the official
Windows amd64 archive at https://broth.itch.zone/butler/windows-amd64/15.32.0/archive/default.
Both native DLLs must remain beside butler.exe. The archive itself contains
no license files; this package includes the upstream component license texts.
